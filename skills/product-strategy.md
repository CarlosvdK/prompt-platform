# Product Strategy

## Vision

Prompt Platform is a marketplace where users discover, preview, and unlock high-quality prompts through an ad-gated access model. The platform combines AI-powered content generation with human editorial oversight to maintain a curated, trustworthy catalog.

## Target Users

### Primary Audiences

- **Developers** -- looking for system prompts, code generation templates, and API integration patterns for LLMs.
- **Content Creators** -- seeking writing prompts, social media templates, and creative brainstorming aids.
- **Marketers** -- searching for copy frameworks, campaign ideation prompts, and audience analysis templates.
- **Educators** -- wanting lesson plan generators, assessment creation prompts, and student engagement tools.

### Secondary Audiences

- **Business professionals** -- meeting agendas, email drafting, decision frameworks.
- **Researchers** -- literature review prompts, hypothesis generation, methodology design.

## Core Value Proposition

1. **Preview-first experience.** Users see representative output before committing to unlock. This builds trust and reduces friction -- users know what they are getting.
2. **Quality-controlled content.** Every prompt passes human review before publication. No auto-published, low-effort content cluttering the catalog.
3. **AI-powered catalog growth.** Internal agents generate prompt drafts at scale, but humans curate what ships. This balances volume with quality.
4. **Free access via ads.** Users pay with attention (watching/interacting with an ad) rather than money. Low barrier to access.

## Key Differentiators

| Differentiator | How we are different |
|---|---|
| Preview-first | Competitors show title + description only. We show actual sample output. |
| Human-reviewed | Most prompt marketplaces are user-generated with no quality gate. |
| Ad-gated (not paywalled) | No subscription or credit system. One ad interaction per unlock. |
| Versioned prompts | Prompts improve over time with tracked version history. |
| Category-organized | Structured taxonomy, not just search and tags. |

## Monetization

- **Primary revenue:** Ad impressions and completions via the unlock flow. Revenue per unlock varies by ad provider, format, and geography.
- **Future revenue streams:** Premium subscriptions (skip ads, bulk unlock), API access for programmatic prompt retrieval, enterprise licensing.

## Success Metrics

| Metric | What it measures |
|---|---|
| Catalog size (published prompts) | Content supply |
| Daily active users | Demand and engagement |
| Unlock rate (unlocks / views) | Conversion and content quality |
| Ad completion rate | Monetization health |
| Review approval rate | Agent generation quality |
| Time to review | Operational efficiency |
| Repeat visit rate | Retention and value delivery |

## Competitive Landscape

The prompt marketplace space includes PromptBase, AIPRM, FlowGPT, and PromptHero. Most rely on user-generated content with minimal curation, subscription paywalls, and no preview system. Our combination of AI generation + human review + ad-gated access + preview-first UX is a distinct positioning.

## Product Principles

1. **Quality over quantity.** A smaller catalog of excellent prompts beats a large catalog of mediocre ones.
2. **Transparency.** Users should always know what they are getting before they commit.
3. **Safety first.** No harmful, misleading, or policy-violating content, ever.
4. **Iterate in public.** Ship incrementally, gather feedback, improve. Do not wait for perfection.
5. **Agents assist, humans decide.** AI accelerates content creation but never bypasses human judgment.
